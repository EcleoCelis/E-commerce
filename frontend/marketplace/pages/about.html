Platform Mission & Workflow

Storefront + Admin

Unlike informal classifieds, SecondMarket validates every stage of a pre-loved purchase—from condition verification and search discovery to stock-safe checkout and shipment tracking.

  • 01
    Transparent Item Grading

    Clear condition tiers (Like New, Good Condition, Fair Value) paired with gallery imagery and seller specs.

  • 02
    Synchronized Buyer Journey

    Persistent wishlists, server-backed carts, multi-channel payments (COD & GCash/E-Wallet), and live order tracking.

  • 03
    Dedicated Admin Seller Console

    Real-time KPI analytics, category and listing lifecycles, inventory protection, fulfillment updates, and CSV exports.
